RICE CULTIVATION GUIDE FOR KERALA

COMMON RICE DISEASES:

1. Rice Leaf Blight (Bacterial Leaf Blight)
- Symptoms: Yellow to white lesions along leaf margins, leaves dry up
- Cause: Bacterial infection, spreads in wet conditions
- Treatment: 
  - Remove and burn affected leaves
  - Apply copper-based fungicides
  - Improve field drainage
  - Use resistant varieties like Jyothi or Uma

2. Rice Blast
- Symptoms: Diamond-shaped spots on leaves, neck rot
- Cause: Fungal infection, spreads in humid weather
- Treatment:
  - Apply tricyclazole or isoprothiolane fungicides
  - Avoid excess nitrogen fertilizer
  - Maintain proper spacing between plants

3. Brown Spot
- Symptoms: Brown oval spots on leaves
- Cause: Fungal infection, worse in nutrient-deficient soils
- Treatment:
  - Apply potassium and zinc fertilizers
  - Use mancozeb or carbendazim fungicides
  - Seed treatment before planting

PEST MANAGEMENT:

1. Stem Borer
- Symptoms: Dead hearts in vegetative stage, white heads at flowering
- Control: 
  - Light traps to monitor
  - Apply carbofuran granules
  - Remove stubbles after harvest

2. Brown Plant Hopper
- Symptoms: Hopper burn, plants turn yellow and dry
- Control:
  - Avoid excess nitrogen
  - Alternate wet and dry irrigation
  - Use neem-based sprays

BEST PRACTICES FOR ALAPPUZHA REGION:

- Best planting season: June-July (Virippu) and November-December (Mundakan)
- Water management: Maintain 2-5 cm water level during vegetative stage
- Fertilizer: Apply NPK 90:45:45 kg/ha in three splits
- Spacing: 20x15 cm for transplanting
- Harvest: When 80% grains turn golden yellow

COCONUT CULTIVATION:

Common Problems:
1. Rhinoceros Beetle
- Hook out beetles from crown
- Fill leaf axils with naphthalene balls and sand
- Use pheromone traps

2. Red Palm Weevil
- Remove and burn affected palms
- Apply carbaryl in crown
- Avoid injuries to palm

3. Root Wilt Disease
- No cure available
- Remove and replant with disease-free seedlings
- Maintain good drainage

BANANA CULTIVATION:

1. Panama Wilt
- Symptoms: Yellowing of older leaves, splitting of pseudostem
- Control: Use disease-free suckers, avoid flood irrigation

2. Bunchy Top Virus
- Symptoms: Narrow, bunched leaves at top
- Control: Remove and destroy infected plants, control aphids

GENERAL FARMING TIPS:

- Always test soil before planting season
- Rotate crops to prevent disease buildup
- Maintain field hygiene - remove crop residues
- Use integrated pest management
- Contact local Krishi Bhavan for specific guidance
- Keep records of pesticide applications
